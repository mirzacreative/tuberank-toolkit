How to Write Better YouTube Titles

Quick answer

Write better YouTube titles by putting your main keyword first, keeping titles under 60 characters, adding a curiosity gap or clear benefit, and matching the title honestly to the video. Test three to five options per video and track click-through rate in YouTube Studio to learn what your audience clicks.

Learn how to write clear, clickable YouTube titles without misleading viewers.

Growing a YouTube channel starts with understanding the viewer. Before writing a title or choosing tags, ask what problem the viewer wants solved and what promise your video can honestly deliver.

How do you write a title people click?

  • Use one clear main keyword naturally in the title.
  • Write a description that summarizes the video and includes related terms.
  • Create a thumbnail that is readable on mobile.
  • Match the hook with the title promise in the first few seconds.
  • Track analytics and improve future videos based on real data.

Do not use spammy keyword stuffing or fake claims. White-hat growth comes from helpful content, clear packaging and consistent publishing.

What makes a title SEO-friendly?

Pick a focused topic, write three title options, create one strong thumbnail concept, add a clear description, and publish with a simple call to action.

Frequently asked questions

Should keywords go at the start of the title?

Yes, when it reads naturally. Front-loading your main keyword helps YouTube and viewers instantly understand the topic, and it survives truncation on mobile.

Can I change a YouTube title after publishing?

Yes, and you should test. If click-through rate is low after a few thousand impressions, try a new title - but keep it honest and give each version time to gather data.

Are numbers in titles effective?

Numbers work well for list and tutorial videos because they set clear expectations, for example 7 Free Tools. Keep the number accurate to the video content.
