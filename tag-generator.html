Frequently asked questions

Do YouTube tags still matter for rankings?

Tags are a minor ranking factor. They help YouTube understand your video topic and catch misspellings, but titles, thumbnails and watch time matter far more.

How many tags should I use?

YouTube allows 500 characters of tags. Use 5 to 15 relevant tags mixing your main keyword, variations and a few broader niche terms.

Should I copy a competitor's tags?

You can use them as inspiration, but write tags that genuinely describe your own video. Irrelevant tags can confuse the algorithm and hurt recommendations.
