How to use this tool

  1. Type a short topic, such as “faceless youtube”. Two or three words work best.
  2. Click Generate to get a comma-separated list of up to 25 tags.
  3. Copy the list and paste it into the tags box in YouTube Studio.
  4. Delete any tag that does not describe your video, and keep the list under YouTube’s 500-character limit.

Worked example

Topic “faceless youtube”. The generator returns 18 tags:

faceless youtube, faceless youtube tips, faceless youtube tutorial, faceless youtube guide, faceless youtube for beginners, faceless youtube 2026, faceless youtube strategy, youtube tips, youtube seo, content creation, creator economy, video marketing, grow youtube channel, faceless, faceless tips, faceless tutorial, youtube, youtube tutorial

Notice the mix: the full phrase, variations of it, single words, and a few broader terms such as “content creation”.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Entering a long sentence as the topic. The generator splits it into single words and fills the list with weak one-word tags such as “how” and “to”. Use a short phrase instead.
  • Keeping every tag without checking. Remove single-word tags that are too broad for your video.
  • Adding tags for topics your video does not cover. Irrelevant tags can confuse recommendations.

Frequently asked questions

Do YouTube tags still matter for rankings?

Tags are a minor ranking factor. They help YouTube understand your video topic and catch misspellings, but titles, thumbnails and watch time matter far more.

How many tags should I use?

YouTube allows 500 characters of tags. Use 5 to 15 relevant tags mixing your main keyword, variations and a few broader niche terms.

Should I copy a competitor's tags?

You can use them as inspiration, but write tags that genuinely describe your own video. Irrelevant tags can confuse the algorithm and hurt recommendations.

Why does the list include single-word tags?

The generator also breaks your topic into single words and adds "tips" and "tutorial" versions of each. Delete the ones that are too broad.

Do tags matter more than the title?

No. Tags are a small signal. Title, thumbnail, and watch time matter far more, so spend most of your effort there.
