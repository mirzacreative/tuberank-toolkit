Frequently asked questions

What is a video hook?

A hook is the first 10 to 15 seconds of your video. Its job is to confirm the title promise and give viewers a reason to keep watching instead of clicking away.

Why do hooks matter for the algorithm?

YouTube promotes videos with strong audience retention. A weak opening makes viewers leave early, which signals the algorithm to recommend your video less.

Should my hook repeat the title?

Restate the promise in your own words rather than reading the title aloud. Then tease the payoff - what the viewer will learn or see - within the first 15 seconds.
