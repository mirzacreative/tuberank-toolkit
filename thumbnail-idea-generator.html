Frequently asked questions

What makes a high-CTR YouTube thumbnail?

High-CTR thumbnails use strong contrast, one clear focal point, readable text of 3 to 4 words or fewer, and an emotion or curiosity gap that matches the title.

How much text should a thumbnail have?

Keep it to 3 to 4 short words. Thumbnails appear small on mobile, so long text becomes unreadable. The text should complement the title, not repeat it.

Should my thumbnail match my title?

They should work as a team: the title and thumbnail together tell one story. Avoid repeating the exact title text on the thumbnail - use the image to add new information or emotion.
