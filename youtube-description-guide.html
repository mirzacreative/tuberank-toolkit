YouTube Description Guide

Quick answer

A strong YouTube description puts a keyword-rich summary in the first 150 characters, adds 150 to 300 words of detail with related terms, and includes timestamps, links and a call to action. YouTube reads descriptions to understand your topic, so clear, honest descriptions support both search rankings and viewer trust.

A simple guide to writing useful descriptions that help viewers and search engines understand your video.

AdSense ad placeholder

Growing a YouTube channel starts with understanding the viewer. Before writing a title or choosing tags, ask what problem the viewer wants solved and what promise your video can honestly deliver.

How do you structure a YouTube description?

  • Use one clear main keyword naturally in the title.
  • Write a description that summarizes the video and includes related terms.
  • Create a thumbnail that is readable on mobile.
  • Match the hook with the title promise in the first few seconds.
  • Track analytics and improve future videos based on real data.

Do not use spammy keyword stuffing or fake claims. White-hat growth comes from helpful content, clear packaging and consistent publishing.

What should every description include?

Pick a focused topic, write three title options, create one strong thumbnail concept, add a clear description, and publish with a simple call to action.

Frequently asked questions

How many keywords should a description have?

Write naturally for viewers first. Mention your main keyword once in the first two sentences and related terms a few times - avoid stuffing the same phrase repeatedly.

Do timestamps help SEO?

Timestamps improve viewer experience and can earn key moments in Google search, which boosts visibility. Add them for any video longer than a few minutes.

Should I put hashtags in the description?

Yes - 3 to 5 relevant hashtags at the end of the description. YouTube displays the first three above your title and ignores all hashtags if you add more than 15.
