How to use this tool

  1. Type your niche or topic — for example “faceless YouTube”.
  2. Click Generate to get 15 video angles for that niche.
  3. Shortlist the ideas that match videos you can actually make this week.
  4. Check each shortlisted idea in YouTube search before filming, to see whether viewers are already looking for it.

Worked example

Niche “faceless YouTube”. The first five of the 15 ideas:

  • Beginner mistakes: faceless YouTube
  • Tools I use: faceless YouTube
  • Step-by-step tutorial: faceless YouTube
  • Myth vs truth: faceless YouTube
  • Case study: faceless YouTube

The generator gives you angles, not finished titles. Turn “Myth vs truth: faceless YouTube” into a specific video, such as the myths you hear most in your niche.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Filming the first idea on the list without checking demand. An angle is only useful if viewers search for it or your audience asks about it.
  • Picking ideas outside your niche. A random idea may get views once, but it will not bring subscribers who want your usual videos.
  • Letting the list go stale. Regenerate ideas when your backlog runs low instead of repeating the same five formats.

Frequently asked questions

How do I pick a video idea that gets views?

Pick ideas that match proven demand in your niche: check what competitors' popular videos cover, read your comments for questions, and validate topics with YouTube search suggestions.

How many video ideas should I plan ahead?

Keep a backlog of at least 10 to 20 ideas. A backlog removes the stress of deciding what to film and lets you batch similar videos together.

Should beginners copy trending formats?

Adapting proven formats to your niche is smart, but add your own angle. Pure copies compete directly with the original; a fresh angle gives viewers a reason to choose you.

How do I turn an angle into a video title?

Add the specific subject. "Checklist: faceless YouTube" becomes "My faceless channel checklist: 7 things before you upload", for example.

What if none of the 15 ideas fit my channel?

Try a narrower niche word. "Cooking" is broad; "budget meals for students" gives you angles you can actually film.
