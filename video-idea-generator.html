Frequently asked questions

How do I pick a video idea that gets views?

Pick ideas that match proven demand in your niche: check what competitors' popular videos cover, read your comments for questions, and validate topics with YouTube search suggestions.

How many video ideas should I plan ahead?

Keep a backlog of at least 10 to 20 ideas. A backlog removes the stress of deciding what to film and lets you batch similar videos together.

Should beginners copy trending formats?

Adapting proven formats to your niche is smart, but add your own angle. Pure copies compete directly with the original; a fresh angle gives viewers a reason to choose you.
