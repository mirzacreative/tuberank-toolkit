Frequently asked questions

How many hashtags should I put on a YouTube video?

Use 3 to 5 relevant hashtags. YouTube shows the first three above your title, and adding more than 15 makes YouTube ignore all of them.

Do hashtags help videos get discovered?

Hashtags help viewers find related videos when clicked, and they signal your topic to YouTube, but they are a small factor compared to title and watch time.

Where do hashtags go on YouTube?

Put them at the end of your video description. YouTube automatically displays the first three above the video title.
