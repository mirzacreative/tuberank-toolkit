How to use this tool

  1. Type your topic, such as “faceless youtube”.
  2. Click Generate to get a space-separated list of hashtags.
  3. Pick three to five hashtags. YouTube shows only the first three above your title.
  4. Paste your picks at the end of your video description.

Worked example

Topic “faceless youtube”. The generator returns:

#YouTube #YouTubeSEO #ContentCreator #CreatorTips #VideoMarketing #GrowOnYouTube #Faceless #Youtube #FacelessYoutube

The first three are the ones viewers will see above the title, so put your most relevant tag first if you reorder them.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Pasting all nine hashtags into the description. More than 15 hashtags makes YouTube ignore all of them, and a long block looks spammy. Pick three to five.
  • Using hashtags that do not match the video. They send viewers to unrelated hashtag pages and waste the space.
  • Putting hashtags in the title. Keep them in the description; the first three still show above the title.

Frequently asked questions

How many hashtags should I put on a YouTube video?

Use 3 to 5 relevant hashtags. YouTube shows the first three above your title, and adding more than 15 makes YouTube ignore all of them.

Do hashtags help videos get discovered?

Hashtags help viewers find related videos when clicked, and they signal your topic to YouTube, but they are a small factor compared to title and watch time.

Where do hashtags go on YouTube?

Put them at the end of your video description. YouTube automatically displays the first three above the video title.

Can I use the same hashtags on every video?

Keep one or two channel hashtags the same, but change the rest to match each video's topic.

Do hashtags work in Shorts?

Yes. Add them to the Short's description the same way, and keep the list short so it does not crowd the description.
