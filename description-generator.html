Frequently asked questions

How long should a YouTube description be?

Aim for 150 to 300 words. Put the most important summary and keywords in the first 150 characters, since only the first lines show before viewers click show more.

Do YouTube descriptions help with SEO?

Yes. YouTube reads your description to understand your video topic, so clear, keyword-rich descriptions can help you rank in search and suggested videos.

Should I include links in my description?

Yes - links to your social profiles, related videos and resources help viewers take action. Add timestamps for longer videos too.
